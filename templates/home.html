About OpenBook

Our Purpose

OpenBook was created to give writers a simple place to share their work and let readers discover new stories for free. We believe that knowledge and creativity should be accessible to everyone.

This platform was built as a final project for the CS50 course at Harvard University, with the goal of creating a safe and legal space for book sharing.

Our Policy

We only accept books with clear licenses:

  • Public Domain works
  • Creative Commons licensed books
  • Original works from the authors themselves

Every upload is reviewed by our team. We do not allow pirated content or books without the author's permission. Users must declare the rights they have over the books they share.
